## Affordable Internet That Supports a Nonprofit Mission

Computers 4 People is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to close the digital divide. The organization accepts donated computers and technology, securely erases the previous owner's data, refurbishes the equipment, and distributes it through nonprofit partners and its computer donation program.

Shield Internet expands that mission beyond hardware. The service uses a cellular connection instead of a cable or fiber line, with hotspot packages and a SIM-only option for compatible devices. ## What to Expect From Shield Internet

Shield can be a practical primary connection for browsing, email, schoolwork, job applications, messaging, and streaming. It may also work well as backup internet during a cable or fiber outage, as long as the local cellular network remains available.

Because the connection travels with the hotspot, it can also be useful for renters, temporary housing, extended stays, road trips, and remote work. There is no permanent line to install, and the service can be moved anywhere compatible cellular coverage is available.

Like any cellular internet service, performance will vary. Signal strength, tower congestion, building materials, hotspot placement, and device capabilities can all affect speed and reliability. A connection that performs extremely well at one address may behave differently only a few miles away.

Place the hotspot near a window or exterior wall and test several locations before choosing a permanent spot. Moving it only a few feet can noticeably improve signal quality. Avoid putting it inside a cabinet, behind a television, or next to equipment that produces heat.

Anyone choosing the SIM-only option should confirm device compatibility before ordering. An unlocked hotspot or cellular router still needs to support the service's network bands, SIM format, and configuration. Unlimited cellular service can also be affected by congestion or network management, so performance may not be identical throughout the day.
